3 · Brew and taste
Make one coffee

      

      
Start your timer when you start the pump. Stop at your target cup weight. Let the coffee cool enough to sip comfortably, then taste it again as it cools.

      

      
      
      
A programmed flow recipe can control the shot time itself. If unsure, leave this unchecked and keep the grind unchanged.

      
Optional tasting notes
A fruity coffee can taste pleasantly acidic. Choose “more sour than I want” only if you want that taste to change. Sour or bitter alone does not tell us how much was extracted.

    Local research archive · remote sharing off
        
    Help improve dial-in guidance with a small record of your shots. Sharing is off until you choose it.
    
        
    Local research archive is on by default; you can turn it off or delete it below. Records include numeric coffee weights, grinder settings and shot times, broad coffee and grinder categories, taste choices and calculation results. They exclude coffee names, personal grinder names, personal device identifiers, credentials, raw timestamps and measurement traces.
    
        
        
        
    Sharing is off. No collector is configured.
    
        
    
        
    
        
    Local export works without a collector or sharing consent. Turning sharing off deletes unsent records from this browser. Random research record and series keys identify corrections and related shots; there is no permanent visitor identifier. Your collector may receive network connection metadata.
