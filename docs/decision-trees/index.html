How the assistant decides

Follow the path from your saved shot to an answer. Each flow explains what the app checks, when it asks for more evidence, and why it may leave a setting unchanged.

A suggestion is a next experiment. These rules do not measure taste, prove extraction quality, or move a grinder. “Abstain” means the available evidence cannot justify this answer. Numerical thresholds are software rules or recovered firmware constants, not universal espresso laws.

Choosing an answer

Choose the kind of answer

The app has separate ways to recall a good shot, suggest a next experiment, describe a saved trace, and replay recovered calculations. A calculated number does not become a machine command.

Taste and the next experiment

Taste, timing and uncertainty

Taste supplies an operator assessment, not a chemical measurement. Tasting temperature and brewing temperature are distinct optional records. The engine never computes a brew-temperature correction from sourness.

View the decision flow
Read the exact conditions
WhenWhat happens
